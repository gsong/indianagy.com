# India Nagy Portfolio

India Nagy's portfolio site: case studies and areas of expertise shown through pictures of her work.

## Language

### Content

**Case study**:
One client project told through sections of pictures and a short description.
_Avoid_: Project, work item

**Expertise**:
One discipline (packaging, illustration, print, …) shown through sections of pictures, structured like a case study.
_Avoid_: Skill, service

**Detail**:
The shared shape of a case study or an expertise: label, highlights, description, sections and card picture.
_Avoid_: Item, entry

**Detail kind**:
Whether a detail is a case study or an expertise. The kind fixes the name shown with the detail, its place in the URL, and its home-page listing.
_Avoid_: Category, type

**Section**:
A labelled group of pictures or videos within a detail. A portfolio picture inside one has the **section** display role.
_Avoid_: Gallery, block

### Pictures

**Portfolio picture**:
Any image on the site that shows India's work or India herself, sized for the place it appears. Its original lives in R2.
_Avoid_: Asset, photo, media

**Display role**:
Where a portfolio picture appears, which fixes how wide it can be shown: **card**, **section** or **portrait**.
_Avoid_: Variant, size, slot

**Card**:
The display role of a detail's tile on a listing page. It is shown at most half the page width.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, tile

**Portrait**:
The display role of India's photo on the about page.
_Avoid_: Headshot, avatar
